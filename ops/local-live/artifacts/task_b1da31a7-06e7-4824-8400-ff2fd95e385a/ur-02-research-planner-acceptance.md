# Research Planner (UR-02) Draft Protocol and Preregistered Analysis Plan

## Title
Research Protocol for Evaluating a Fictional Local-First Personal Planning Product

## Research Objective
To evaluate the usability, user experience, and perceived value of a fictional one-week personal planning exercise designed for local-first personal planning.

## Research Questions
1. How do users perceive the effectiveness of the one-week planning exercise in helping them organize their personal goals and tasks?
2. What are the key challenges users face when using the planning exercise?
3. How does the local-first design of the product influence user engagement and satisfaction?

## Hypotheses
1. Users will report a positive experience with the one-week planning exercise, particularly in terms of goal clarity and task prioritization.
2. Users will identify usability challenges related to the local-first design, such as difficulty in accessing or sharing local resources.
3. The local-first design will be perceived as more engaging and relevant compared to a generic planning tool.

## Tasks
1. Participants will complete the one-week planning exercise using the fictional product.
2. Participants will complete a post-exercise survey assessing their experience, perceived value, and challenges.
3. Participants will be asked to reflect on how the local-first design influenced their engagement with the product.

## Protocol
- **Recruitment**: Identify and recruit 20 participants who are interested in personal planning and have access to local resources.
- **Informed Consent**: Participants will be provided with an informed consent form outlining the purpose, procedures, and potential risks of the study.
- **Session Structure**:
  - Pre-exercise survey (10 minutes)
  - One-week planning exercise (7 days)
  - Post-exercise survey (15 minutes)
- **Data Collection**: All data will be collected through surveys and self-reported feedback.

## Sample
- **Size**: 20 participants
- **Inclusion Criteria**:
  - Age 18+ years
  - Interest in personal planning
  - Access to local resources
- **Exclusion Criteria**:
  - No interest in personal planning
  - No access to local resources
  - Prior experience with similar planning tools (to be verified)

## Analysis Plan
- **Quantitative Analysis**:
  - Descriptive statistics for survey responses
  - Thematic analysis of open-ended responses
- **Qualitative Analysis**:
  - Thematic coding of user feedback
  - Identification of common challenges and perceived benefits
- **Inferential Limits**:
  - Due to the small sample size, inferential statistics will be limited to descriptive analysis and thematic insights.
  - No statistical generalization will be made to a larger population.

## Success Criteria
- Completion of the one-week planning exercise
- Completion of the post-exercise survey
- Clear and actionable feedback from participants

## Boundaries and Unknowns
- **Unknowns**:
  - The exact features and functionality of the fictional product are not provided.
  - The definition of "local-first" is not clearly defined.
  - The exact nature of "local resources" is not specified.
- **Assumptions**:
  - The fictional product is designed to be used in a local context.
  - Participants will have access to the necessary tools and resources to complete the planning exercise.
  - The planning exercise is intended to be used over a one-week period.

## Evidence and Assumptions
- **Assumptions**:
  - The fictional product is functional and accessible to participants.
  - Participants will be able to complete the planning exercise within the one-week timeframe.
  - Participants will provide honest and thoughtful feedback.
- **Evidence**:
  - The study is based on user-reported experiences and feedback.
  - The analysis will be based on self-reported data and thematic insights.

## Final Notes
This is a draft protocol and analysis plan. Further details about the fictional product, its features, and the local-first design must be verified and clarified before proceeding with recruitment and data collection.
