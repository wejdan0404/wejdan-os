# Research Program and Study Sign-Off Draft – UR-01

## Research Agenda
The research agenda focuses on understanding user experiences with a fictional local-first personal planning product. The core objective is to evaluate how users engage with a one-week planning exercise, identify pain points, and uncover insights that can inform product improvements. The study will explore user behavior, motivations, and challenges in the context of a local-first approach to personal planning.

## Research Method
**Evaluative Method** is selected for this study. This method is appropriate for assessing user experience with a specific product feature (the one-week planning exercise) and gathering actionable insights to guide product development.

## Sample Logic
- **Target Population**: Users of the fictional local-first personal planning product.
- **Sample Size**: 15 users (pending verification by the Product Team).
- **Sampling Method**: Purposeful sampling to include a mix of user types (e.g., frequent planners, occasional planners, new users).
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Users who have engaged with the one-week planning exercise at least once.
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Users who have not used the planning feature or have not provided feedback previously.

## Ethical Process
- **Informed Consent**: All participants will be provided with a clear explanation of the study purpose, procedures, and their rights.
- **Data Anonymization**: All user data will be anonymized to protect participant privacy.
- **Right to Withdraw**: Participants may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
- **Data Security**: Data will be stored securely and only used for research purposes.
- **Ethical Review**: The study will be reviewed by the internal ethics committee (pending confirmation by the Legal Team).

## Synthesis Standard
- **Data Collection**: Qualitative data will be collected through interviews, observations, and user feedback.
- **Analysis**: Thematic analysis will be used to identify patterns and insights.
- **Reporting**: Findings will be synthesized into a report that includes key themes, user quotes, and actionable recommendations.
- **Stakeholder Alignment**: Findings will be shared with the Product Team and other stakeholders to inform product decisions.

## Stakeholder Communication
- **Internal Communication**: Findings will be shared with the Product Team, UX Team, and Leadership via a summary report and presentation.
- **External Communication**: No external communication is planned at this stage.
- **Timeline**: Communication will occur within two weeks of study completion.

## Product Implications
Findings will be routed to PROD-01 for consideration in future product updates and feature development. Specific implications will be identified during the synthesis phase and shared with the Product Team.

## Evidence and Assumptions
- **Assumption 1**: The one-week planning exercise is a core feature of the product and is used by a significant portion of the user base.
- **Assumption 2**: User feedback will be available and accessible for analysis.
- **Assumption 3**: The Product Team will confirm the sample size and sampling criteria.
- **Assumption 4**: The Legal Team will confirm the ethical review process.

## Boundaries and Unknowns
- **Unknown 1**: Exact user demographics and engagement levels with the planning feature.
- **Unknown 2**: The full scope of the product’s local-first features and how they interact with the planning exercise.
- **Unknown 3**: The exact timeline for the study and the availability of the Product Team for stakeholder communication.
- **Unknown 4**: The final ethical review process and approval status.

## Status
This is a **draft** of the research program and study sign-off. Further verification and approval from the Product Team and Legal Team are required before finalization.
