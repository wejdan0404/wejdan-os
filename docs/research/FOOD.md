- Krop 2018 oral-processing meta-analysis (PMID 29408331) - relevant but redundant with Robinson 2014; not used.
- Saudi MOH vaccination page (moh.gov.sa .../vaccination/Pages/003.aspx) - fetched; mostly child/pregnancy content, no adult schedule. Replaced by the Saudi Clinical Preventive Guideline 2023 (Public Health Authority PDF, adult vaccine Table 2).
- Saudi MOH seasonal-flu FAQ page (moh.gov.sa/en/flu/pages/qa.aspx) - 404.
- AAD "when to see a dermatologist" hair-loss page - 404; used AAD "Hair loss: signs and symptoms" instead.
- Monash PCOS Guideline Summary PDF - URL returned HTML, not PDF; used PubMed abstract (PMID 37580861) + JCEM full text (lifestyle section) instead.
- Saudi Healthy Plate-2024 (DOI 10.1186/s40795-025-01146-7) - title/journal verified via Crossref, but content not read, so no claims taken from it; listed only as a reference that the 2013 Healthy Food Palm is being updated.
- ACOG "menstrual cycle as a vital sign" - adolescent population, excluded per spec.

GAPS / WEAK EVIDENCE
- Meal planning, grocery lists, home cooking: cross-sectional/observational only (grocery-list study population = low-income US food-desert residents). No dose from any source.
- Food-environment Cochrane review (2019): GRADE low/very low certainty, mostly lab studies.
- Self-monitoring: benefit evidence is from weight-loss programs (weak level); harm signal (calorie trackers linked to eating concern in college students) is cross-sectional. Kept as Optional with explicit stop rule; recommend habit checkboxes, not calorie counting.
- Regular meals: AHA scientific statement (expert review), not a trial-graded recommendation. Breakfast not required (BMJ 2019 meta-analysis).
- Protein: ISSN 1.4-2.0 g/kg/d is a sports-society position stand (some industry-affiliated authors); Morton 2018 SR/MA (~1.6 g/kg/d plateau) is the stronger source. No women-specific figure found.
- Skincare/hair: AAD pages are professional-body expert guidance, not graded guidelines. No official evidence for specific skincare products/actives beyond sunscreen, gentle cleanser, moisturizer.
- Iron: NIH ODS (US) used; no Saudi-specific iron guidance verified.
- Saudi Healthy Food Palm: only the 8 key messages (via FAO record of the 2013 MOH guideline) were verified; no Saudi portion numbers used.
- Page dates for AAD, ADA, CDC, OWH, NIH ODS pages were not confirmed; their "year" field = access year 2026.

SURPRISING / USEFUL FOR THE APP
- The Saudi Clinical Preventive Guideline (3rd ed., 2023) gives a clear adult checklist usable for the app's "health admin" reminders: BP every 3-5 years for 18-39 not at increased risk (annually if at risk), BMI & waist every 2 years, blood sugar from 35 (earlier with risk factors), influenza annually, Tdap then Td every 10 years, HPV catch-up for females 15-26. Cervical screening is cited via USPSTF criteria (eligibility decided by the clinician) - phrased neutrally in FOOD-24.
- WHO now recommends at least 25 g/day naturally occurring fibre (2023 carbohydrate guideline), and the WHO fact sheet (updated Jan 2026) confirms free sugars <10% with additional benefit at <=5%.
- Implementation intentions work better for ADDING healthy foods (d~0.51) than for cutting unhealthy ones (d~0.29) - supports "add, don't ban" framing in the app.
- Distracted eating raised later intake more than immediate intake - screen-free meals is a strong, cheap Core habit.
- Hydration: only the nutrition swap (water instead of sugary drinks, FOOD-03) is included; general fluid targets left to the BODY cluster.
- Menstrual and PCOS data are sensitive health data: recommend local/encrypted storage and PCOS module hidden unless she enables it.
