### Checked and not defects
- **Selected state of segmented controls, energy cards and chips.** Exposed as `aria-current=true/false` via Flutter semantics `selected`, so VoiceOver/TalkBack can announce it.
- **Sparks cap 20.** Holds across 15 check/uncheck cycles. Free joy = 0. Weekly review +5 is paid once per week. Controller test passes.
- **Reminders.** Cap is respected for caps 0/1/2/4/6 and nothing falls inside quiet hours (engine test passes). The cap stepper clamps at 6.
- **Strength stage.** 35 consecutive done days with no SRBAI check stays at or below "growing" (not day-count based).
- **SRS intervals.** 1 → 6 → about 15 days; a lapse is due tomorrow; ease floor 1.3.
- **IELTS rotation.** Sat R, Sun L, Mon W, Tue S, Thu = weakest.
- **Planner caps.** Hold on all 7 weekdays for Green/Yellow/Red, and the budget stays ≤ ×0.7.
- **Fresh start.** Detected on the 1st of the month.
- **RTL mirroring.** Tab order, back/forward chevrons and progress direction are correct in AR. EN mirrors to LTR correctly.
- **Digits.** No Eastern Arabic digits in any semantics label across 75 screens.
- **English leaking into AR.** Only in source titles (English originals), "IELTS Speaking Part 2", URLs, and D17.

## 5. Evidence integrity

- **UI numbers vs sources.** `tool/qa/evidence_numbers.py` → `docs/qa/evidence_numbers.txt`.
  - Every number in `actions_ar.json` fields appears in the same action's English record (0 mismatches over 135 actions).
  - A strict field-to-field pass found 4 cases, all English number words rendered as digits ("Three" → 3, "quarterly" → "كل 3 شهور", "two minutes" → "2 د"). These are acceptable.
  - ARB strings with numbers are either spec design values (caps 3/5, 0–6, 25/50, 4 weeks, SRBAI 1–5, band 0–9) or help-line numbers. App-invented durations are D07.
- **Help lines.**
  - **937:** MOH 937 page (https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/937/pages/default.aspx) says "(937) service center 24/7" and "Provide 24/7 medical consultation through doctors". Matches the app's "24 ساعة". ✓
  - **920033360:** MOH psychiatry page (https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/Ministry/Information-and-services/Pages/psychiatry.aspx) says the call center "receives, via its number: 920033360 … psychological consultations by trained and experienced mental health professionals", open "12 hours a day (from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.), except on Saturdays from 1:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m." ✓ The number matches. The app says "hours per the MOH page", which is fine; it could show the hours.
  - **997 (Red Crescent) and 911 (unified): not verified from an official page in this session.**
    - my.gov.sa (national portal, emergency numbers) returned 403.
    - srca.org.sa reset the connection.
    - A secondary source, Saudipedia (https://saudipedia.com/en/article/710/…), confirms the 911 National Center for Security Operations exists in Makkah (2014), Riyadh (2020) and the Eastern Province (2022). It says nothing about 997.
    - Neither number is in `actions.json` sources (D13). The owner should verify both on my.gov.sa and srca.org.sa and add them as sources.
